Privacy Policy — Tummy Scanner

Last updated: 8 August 2026
The short version: Tummy Scanner collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Everything it remembers stays on your device, and uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

What the app does

Tummy Scanner is a children's game. It shows your phone's camera view styled to look like an ultrasound and draws cartoon worms over it, to help explain to a child how sugary food affects their tummy. It is not a medical device, it does not detect anything inside anyone, and it emits no ultrasound.

Information we collect

None. We operate no servers and receive no data from the app whatsoever.

The camera

The app asks for camera permission so it can show the live picture and draw over it. The camera image is processed entirely on your device, in memory, to position the cartoon. It is never uploaded, transmitted or shared, and no photo or video is saved unless you press the save button yourself.

What is stored on your device

Held in the app's own local storage, and readable only by the app:

  • The names and icons you enter for each child.
  • Which foods were ticked, and the resulting worm count and daily streak, for the last 30 days.
  • Up to 8 saved scan pictures, kept only when you press the save button.
  • Your detection-tuning settings.

None of this leaves the device. To erase it, delete individual saved scans and profiles inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything at once.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or registration.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No network requests — the app works fully offline.
  • No location, contacts, microphone or file access.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected.

Children

The app is intended to be used by a parent or carer together with their child. Because it collects no personal information at all, there is nothing to disclose to or about a child under applicable children's privacy laws, including COPPA and the GDPR. Names you type for profiles are entirely your choice and never leave the phone — a nickname or initial is perfectly sufficient.

Permissions we request

  • Camera — to show the live picture the game is drawn over. Required.
  • Vibrate — short taps of haptic feedback. Optional.
